# Obon yōkai artwork

The three `obon-yokai-*-v1.webp` files are original transparent illustrations
generated for this site with OpenAI ImageGen on 2026-10-23. They were generated
as paired, full-body cutouts and converted lossily to 960 × 640 WebP at quality
84 with lossless alpha. No third-party image was copied into the output.

## Art direction

- Premium modern Japanese festival-poster illustration.
- Ink outlines, layered watercolour and subtle washi texture.
- Indigo, vermilion, moss green and antique-gold palette.
- Friendly, mysterious Obon night parade; no gore, text, logos, trademarks,
  emojis, pixel art, or modern copyrighted characters.
- The paired subjects are kappa + karakasa-kozō, chōchin-obake +
  hitotsume-kozō, and kitsune festival dancer + tengu.

## Public-domain research references

These sources were used to check traditional names and historical visual
language; their scans are not embedded in the generated assets.

- National Diet Library, *Toriyama Sekien's Illustrated Night Parade of the
  Demon Horde*: https://www.ndl.go.jp/en/imagebank/column/sekienyokai
- Japan Search, *Gazu Hyakki Yakō*, marked Public Domain:
  https://jpsearch.go.jp/en/item/tokyomuseumcolection-edo_tokyo_museumjbD10000008
- Smithsonian Libraries, *Gazu Hyakki yagyō*, public domain:
  https://library.si.edu/digital-library/book/gazu-hyakki-yagyo
- Wikimedia Commons, public-domain *Gazu Hyakki yagyō* scan:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gazu_Hyakki_yagy%C5%8D_(IA_GazuHyakkiyagyo1Tori).pdf

The site deliberately avoids *Yo-kai Watch*, *GeGeGe no Kitarō*, and any
recognisable modern anime, game, film, or commercial mascot design.
